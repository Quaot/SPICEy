How to use it

Drawing

Pick a part on the left and click next to a point in the circuit. The part starts from that point and faces the way it most likely should, which a faint preview shows before you click. A click on a wire joins the part to the middle of it. To choose both ends yourself, drag between two grid points instead.

Hover over a diode or a source and click Reverse to turn it around. Select a part to change its value, or drag it to move it.

Running

Click a switch or a logic input to flip it. With the probe tool, click a point to plot its voltage, or a part to plot its current.

Wires are orange above ground and blue below it. The dots move the way current flows.

Moving around

Drag empty space to pan and scroll to zoom. F fits the circuit to the screen.

Each step solves the circuit with modified nodal analysis. Capacitors and inductors use the trapezoidal rule, and diodes and the regulator are found by iteration. The source is on GitHub.
