Kirchhoff

A circuit simulator. Wires are coloured by voltage, orange above ground and blue below, and the moving dots show which way current flows and roughly how much.

Place a part
Pick it on the left, then drag from one grid point to another. A click places it at the default size.
Edit
Select a part to change its value. Drag a part to move it, or drag one of its ends.
Switches and inputs
Click them to flip them.
Scope
With the probe tool, click a point to plot its voltage or a part to plot its current.
Move around
Drag empty space to pan and scroll to zoom. F fits the circuit to the screen.
Keys
W wire, R resistor, C capacitor, L inductor, V source, I current source, D diode, E LED, S switch, G ground, P supply, N logic input, O probe. Delete removes, Ctrl+Z undoes, Space pauses.

It solves the circuit with modified nodal analysis, using the trapezoidal rule for capacitors and inductors and Newton's method for diodes. The source is on GitHub.
